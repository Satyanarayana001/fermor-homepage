# Fermor — Homepage

> A clearer, calmer way to understand, act and grow financially.

A responsive homepage concept for Fermor, created as part of the frontend assignment.

The design combines editorial typography, clean minimalism, and bento-style financial UI to make a finance product feel approachable, modern, and trustworthy.

## Design Approach

The goal was to avoid creating another generic fintech dashboard.

Instead, the homepage is built around three core ideas:

### Understand
Give users a clear picture of their financial life without overwhelming them.

### Act
Turn financial information into practical next steps and meaningful goals.

### Grow
Help users build better habits and make consistent progress over time.

This thinking is reflected throughout the page through clear hierarchy, spacious layouts, financial visualizations, and focused calls to action.

---

## Visual Direction

The visual system combines:

- Editorial typography for personality and hierarchy
- Minimal layouts for clarity
- Bento-style cards for financial information
- Subtle glassmorphism for depth
- Deep green and warm neutral palette for trust and calm
- Subtle motion and hover interactions
- Rounded surfaces with restrained shadows

The intention was to make finance feel less intimidating and more human.

## Product Thinking

The homepage was intentionally designed around clarity rather than feature density.

Instead of presenting users with a complex financial dashboard immediately, the experience progressively introduces:

Context → Understanding → Action → Progress

This keeps the first interaction simple while still communicating the depth of the product.
